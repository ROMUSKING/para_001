## 1. What this run is

This is the confirmatory 5-seed execution of **Milestone E2.2 / Track B Milestone B2** (pilot v2) on the official 100-episode DROID dataset (`droid_100`, RLDS). It implements the fixed evaluation contract defined in [`docs/plans/rival-benchmark-plan.md`](../plans/rival-benchmark-plan.md) and addresses all protocol defects identified in pilot v1 ([findings note](2026-09-29-droid100-pilot-findings.md) §2.4):

- **Data and Splits:** 80 train / 10 validation / 10 test episodes, split by SHA-256 episode hash before windowing (5,910 train / 796 validation / 835 test windows per seed; 4,175 pooled test windows across 5 seeds). Window parameters: 8 context steps, 4 horizon steps at frame stride 2.
- **Contract fixes:**
  - Candidate 0 is explicitly `hold` (zero effect $\delta_0 = 0$, zero cost $c_0 = 0$).
  - Stage 1 training matches evaluation semantics (single-choice perturbation applied uniformly).
  - Baselines include exact expected random, `always_hold`, and `always_c_k` for all $k \in \{1..4\}$.
  - Separate parameter-matched heads for amortised co-state (`allocator_costate`), direct critic (`allocator_critic`), and selective invocation (`allocator_gate`), trained with distinct optimisers on frozen dynamics teachers.
  - Evaluation traces store full `exact_gain[N, K+1]` matrices in `artifacts/allocation_traces_v2.parquet` (335 KB).
- **Dynamics Gate:** Revised per k-fold findings ([research note](2026-10-01-dynamics-kfold.md)) to require multi-step terminal horizon relative improvement ($h=4$ improvement $\ge 0.02$) or horizon-mean relative improvement $\ge 0.02$.
- **Model:** 27.36 M trainable parameters dynamics substrate (ResNet-18 visual embeddings, 14-D proprio state, 7-D actions).

## 4. Interpretation

### 4.1 The Mechanism Principle Holds (`exact_costate` = 0.00026)
The exact autograd co-state oracle achieves a test regret of **0.00026**, dramatically outperforming all fixed baselines (`always_hold` at 0.00814) and all learned policies. This demonstrates conclusively that first-order co-state guidance $-\langle \lambda, \delta_k \rangle - c_k$ is mathematically sound on this latent dynamics representation: true gradients $\nabla_z J$ identify optimal refinements with near-zero error.

### 4.2 Why the Run is Classified as `NON_DIAGNOSTIC`
Per governing protocol [`docs/research-plan/adjoint_guided_comprehensive_research_plan.md`](../research-plan/adjoint_guided_comprehensive_research_plan.md) and [`docs/plans/rival-benchmark-plan.md`](../plans/rival-benchmark-plan.md) §5:
1. **Low Refinement Opportunity:** The fixed baseline `always_hold` achieves a mean regret of **0.00814**. Refinements on DROID-100 rarely offer sufficient objective gain to offset their operational cost ($c_k \in [0.002, 0.004]$). The oracle prefers `hold` on the vast majority of windows.
2. **Opportunity Gate Failure:** For all 5 seeds, the validation opportunity gate returned `False`. The headroom of dynamic allocation over the best fixed policy (`always_hold`) was $<15\%$ of the headroom over random. When a benchmark provides near-zero adaptive headroom, it cannot serve as a diagnostic test bed for distinguishing between allocators.
3. **No Co-state Specificity in Amortised Head:** The randomized control $R_{\text{randomized}} - R_{\text{adjoint}} = -0.00023$ (95% CI $[-0.0015, +0.0002]$) shows that the learned neural co-state head $\hat{\lambda}$ does not convey direction-specific co-state information beyond random vectors of similar norm.

## 6. What this Run Does and Does Not Support

- **Supports:**
  - First-order adjoint scoring is mathematically valid on this substrate: exact autograd co-states reduce regret to 0.00026.
  - The revised terminal-horizon dynamics gate is robust and reproducible across 5 random seeds ($100\%$ pass rate).
  - The direct critic learns meaningful value: it beats the uncertainty heuristic ($R_{\text{critic}} - R_{\text{uncertainty}} = -0.0290$, 95% CI $[-0.0396, -0.0145]$).
  - The NVIDIA L4 provides sufficient compute and memory bandwidth for real-time inference (0.27 ms).

- **Does NOT Support:**
  - Any claim of empirical superiority of learned amortised adjoint allocators over direct critics on DROID-100 ($R_{\text{adjoint}} - R_{\text{critic}} = +0.0149$, CI brackets zero).
  - Any paper claims asserting working co-state guidance in production on DROID-100.
  - The current candidate perturbation set as an adaptive allocation benchmark (`always_hold` dominates).
