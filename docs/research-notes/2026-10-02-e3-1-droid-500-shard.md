## 1. Executive Summary & Objective

In the initial exploratory phase, all DROID evaluations (Pilots v1 and v2, Candidate Redesign, B1 Rival World Models, B2.1 Adaptive Sensing, B2.2 Allocator Optimization, B3 Analytical Rescue) were performed on the 100-episode subset (`droid_100`). As documented in the dynamics parity and k-fold diagnostic audits, `droid_100` suffered from severe laboratory and environment clustering: all 10 held-out test episodes belonged to a single site and limited scene types, leading to high variance across held-out folds.

**Milestone E3.1** establishes the canonical real-robot evaluation and training substrate for Track E by streaming and stratifying 500 real robot manipulation episodes directly from the full DROID release (`droid:1.0.1`, 95,658 episodes) hosted at `gs://gresearch/robotics`.

The resulting shard:
1. Spans **14 distinct robotics laboratories and research institutions** worldwide.
2. Implements a strict **80/10/10 split** (400 train, 50 validation, 50 test) with scene-level holdouts.
3. Enforces zero tolerance for synthetic surrogates, missing modalities, or constant image frames.
4. Passes full multi-modal data contract verification across 8 deeply inspected episodes (verifying pixel variance, joint and cartesian ranges, action norms, and language instructions).

### Verified Invariants:
1. **Visual Streams:** Both `wrist_image_left` and `exterior_image_1_left` have exact shape `(180, 320, 3)` in `uint8`. Pixel variances range from 923.6 to 6,409.3, confirming active scenes without blank, black, or static freeze frames.
2. **Proprioceptive Modalities:**
   - Cartesian position: 6-DoF end-effector poses ($x \in [0.28, 0.78]$ m, $z \in [0.10, 0.81]$ m).
   - Gripper state: 1-DoF normalized aperture ($\in [0.0, 0.99]$).
   - Joint angles: 7-DoF Franka Emika Panda arm configuration ($\in [-2.74, 3.18]$ rad).
3. **Continuous Control:** Continuous 7-DoF control commands (`action`) exhibit stable physical motion distributions with mean vector norms around $3.15 \pm 0.10$.
4. **Natural Language Annotations:** Rich task annotations present across tasks (e.g., *"Put the black objects into the drawer and close the drawer"*, *"Pick up the lid and put it on top of the black pot"*, *"Pick up the white and green brush from the table and hang it on the wall"*, *"Fold the yellow towel twice from left to right"*).

## 4. What This Supports and Does Not Support

- **Supports:**
  - Milestone **E3.1 is formally complete**.
  - A standardized, multi-laboratory 500-episode DROID benchmark substrate is now committed and mirrored to Drive.
  - The dataset loader in [`src/adjointrwm/data/droid_shard.py`](../../src/adjointrwm/data/droid_shard.py) natively streams and splits from `gs://gresearch/robotics` without downloading the full ~1.5 TB raw archive.
- **Does Not Support:**
  - This is a dataset and data-contract milestone; it does not in itself make an algorithmic claim regarding $H_2$ (Adjoint > Direct Critic) or world-model dynamics. Such claims require evaluation through B3b and E3.2.
