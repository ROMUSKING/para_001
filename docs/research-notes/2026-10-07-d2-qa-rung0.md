oracle 0.0291, BM25 0.3129, TF-IDF 0.3488, random-expected ~0.65 (grid mean).

## 2. Reading

Retrieval opportunity exists (G1) but question-text rankers leave three-quarters of
it on the table (G2). Per the frozen plan this routes to candidate redesign — e.g.
decomposition-aware or multi-hop-aware retrieval — not to training a learned ranker
on this signal. A reader-aware Rung-1 needs a separate reviewed design with answer
endpoints; nothing here licences it.
